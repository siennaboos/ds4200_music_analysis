Understanding Music Popularity Across Genres

This visualization explores average popularity scores across our groupings of genres. The popularity metric ranges from 0 to 100 and captures how frequently tracks are played and how recently. Brazilian music leads with the highest score of 44.3. Pop follows at 42.1, maintaining its universally accessible appeal, while Asian Pop & Regional ranks third at 38.9, reflecting K-pop and J-pop's global rise. What's particularly intriguing is mood-based and ambient music scoring 38.9, suggesting listeners are intentionally curating their auditory environment for psychological purposes like studying, sleeping, or finding calm. This connects directly to mental health and music psychology: the high popularity of mood-specific genres indicates people are increasingly using music as a tool for emotional regulation and self-care. Meanwhile, Jazz & Blues and Classical show lower scores, serving more specialized audiences who use them for contemplation and deep emotional processing. Understanding which genres resonate broadly helps us recognize how different musical traditions serve different psychological needs, whether it's Brazilian music's communal energy fostering connection, Pop's familiar comfort reducing anxiety, or ambient music promoting relaxation and focus for mental wellness.
